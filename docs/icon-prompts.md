# Geschilderde spelknoppen

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool, met transparante achtergrond. De geselecteerde PNG's zijn voor het spel verkleind naar 512 × 512 WebP, met behoud van transparantie.

Opgeslagen projectbestanden:
- `assets/home-painted.webp`
- `assets/computer-painted.webp`
- `assets/music-painted.webp`

## Gedeelde prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Production game UI asset for a warm Dutch preschool forest counting game, ages 4-5. Rich contemporary painted storybook illustration matching orange squirrel and hedgehog portraits, amber acorns and lush forest foliage; polished hand-painted textures, softly shaded, warm amber wood and fresh green leaves, simple strong readable silhouette at 80px. One isolated centered icon, square composition with small safe margin, actual transparent background, no text, no watermark, no surrounding scene, no drop shadow outside object.

## Home

Subject: a welcoming tiny woodland tree-stump cottage with rounded golden wood door, warm glowing little window and a rounded moss-and-leaf roof. Charming storybook home button, substantial three dimensional wooden volume, not a flat outlined house pictogram. Front three-quarter view.

## Computer

Subject: a friendly small wooden desktop computer with thick rounded honey wood frame, moss green screen displaying two kind expressive eyes and a tiny smile, sturdy small wood foot, one small leaf growing at the corner. Recognizable computer opponent for preschoolers, no arms, no legs, not a robot, no keyboard. Front view, well spaced self-contained object.

## Muziek

Subject: a round wooden music medallion, thick irregular amber wood bark rim, pale honey wood inset, one large chunky dark forest-green pair of connected musical eighth notes carved in relief. Three small fresh leaves tucked at upper right edge. Symmetric front view. No slash, no pause/play symbol, no controls, only large clear music notes.
